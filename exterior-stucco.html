SYSTEM BREAKDOWN

WHAT IS EIFS STUCCO?

Exterior Insulation and Finish System (EIFS) is a non-load bearing exterior wall cladding system that provides buildings with an insulated, water-resistant, finished surface in an integrated composite material assembly.

Unlike traditional 3-coat cement plaster which rests directly against framing, modern EIFS creates a continuous layer of insulation outside the structural studs, eliminating thermal bridges and wrapping the building in a thermal blanket.

THE 6-LAYER EIFS ANATOMY

  1. 01
    Substrate & Sheathing Plywood, OSB, or glass-mat gypsum board base.
  2. 02
    Liquid Air/Vapor Barrier Elastomeric membrane prevents moisture migration into wall cavity.
  3. 03
    EPS Insulation Board Expanded polystyrene boards attached with adhesive or mechanical fasteners.
  4. 04
    Reinforced Polymer Base Coat Polymer-modified cementitious base coat layer.
  5. 05
    Fiberglass Mesh Reinforcement High-impact alkali-resistant woven mesh embedded into base coat.
  6. 06
    100% Acrylic Finish Coat Tinted elastomeric coat providing UV resistance, texture, and weatherproofing.
THERMAL ADVANTAGE

CONTINUOUS INSULATION (CI).

Standard wood or steel stud framing conducts heat out of your building through thermal bridging. By placing continuous EPS insulation on the exterior facade, EIFS increases wall assembly R-value exponentially, dramatically reducing annual heating gas and cooling electric bills.

UP TO 30% ENERGY SAVINGS

EIFS out-performs conventional brick or siding assemblies in thermal wall resistance under Ontario Energy Code standards.

SURFACE TECHNOLOGY

ACRYLIC SURFACE FINISHES.

Our finish coats utilize 100% acrylic polymers infused with dirt-pickup-resistant (DPR) technology. The flexible acrylic resin breathes out internal moisture vapor while blocking external rain droplets from entering.

UV FADE-RESISTANT PIGMENTS

Custom color pigments engineered to maintain color vibrancy for decades without chalking or flaking.

APPLICATION & CARE GUIDELINES.

How Reno Sons installs and maintains premium exterior stucco wall systems for peak performance.

01. PREPARATION

SUBSTRATE INSPECTION

We inspect wall framing, install starter tracks, apply liquid weather barrier membranes, and integrate window/door flashing detail.

02. APPLICATION

PRECISION LAYERING

EPS boards are rasped flat, fiberglass mesh is hand-troweled into polymer base coat, and acrylic texture coat is floated cleanly.

03. MAINTENANCE

LONG-TERM CARE

Low-pressure gentle washing every 3-5 years keeps acrylic surfaces dirt-free. Inspect sealant joints annually around windows and doors.
