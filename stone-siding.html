STONE CATEGORIES

CRAFTED MASONRY SOLUTIONS.

Stone siding adds undeniable permanence, rich organic texture, and architectural weight to your building's facade.

At Reno Sons Stucco, our stonemasons specialize in applying thin-cut natural stone veneer, manufactured architectural stone, and traditional ashlar masonry using specialized polymer mortars and moisture drainage systems.

OUR STONE PROFILE MATRIX

01. STACKED LEDGESTONE

Linear cut stones stacked horizontally for a crisp modern shadow line.

02. THIN NATURAL GRANITE VENEER

Real quarried granite sliced to 1.25" thickness for low weight and maximum durability.

03. ARCHITECTURAL LIMESTONE SILLS & TRIM

Smooth-faced Indiana limestone sills, portico arches, and pillar caps.

ARCHITECTURAL HARMONY

SEAMLESS STUCCO & STONE INTEGRATION.

Combining warm, textured stone masonry with smooth acrylic stucco creates visual hierarchy and timeless luxury.

We anchor the foundation and entrance porticos with stone, while wrapping upper elevations in high-R value EIFS stucco. Our waterproofing flashing details guarantee zero water leaks at stone-to-stucco transition lines.

MASONRY SPECIFICATIONS

PRECISION MASONRY INSTALLATION.

Our 4-step stone veneer installation method prevents moisture damage and guarantees structural adhesion.

01

WRB MEMBRANE

Dual-layer weather resistant barrier applied over exterior sheathing.

02

EXPANDED LATH

03

SCRATCH COAT
