DISCIPLINE 01

EXTERIOR STUCCO (EIFS).

Exterior Insulation and Finish Systems (EIFS) provide an integrated weather barrier and continuous exterior thermal insulation envelope.

Our EIFS installations feature high-density EPS insulation board, mechanical fastening systems, heavy-duty fiberglass reinforcement mesh, polymer scratch coats, and 100% elastomeric acrylic finishes engineered for Canadian winters.

DISCIPLINE 02

NATURAL & CULTURED STONE SIDING.

Custom stone veneer, natural granite ledge, and architectural limestone features that anchor your building with timeless texture.

We seamlessly integrate stone siding with stucco wall cladding, creating striking architectural contrast on entry porticos, lower foundation bands, pillar features, and full exterior elevations.

STUCCO FINISHES & ACRYLIC TEXTURES.

From subtle sand float finishes to bold scraped heavy textures and smooth contemporary acrylics.

STUCCO REPAIR & MOISTURE REMEDIATION.

Expert diagnosis and repair of hairline cracks, impact damage, water infiltration, and delaminated plaster.
