STUCCO & STONE FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

EIFS (Exterior Insulation and Finish System) is a multi-layered exterior wall cladding system that includes continuous EPS insulation board, a liquid-applied air/water barrier membrane, a fiberglass mesh reinforced base coat, and a 100% acrylic finish coat. Unlike traditional 3-coat cement plaster which rests directly against studs, EIFS wraps the exterior of your building in continuous insulation, drastically improving R-value energy efficiency and eliminating thermal bridges.

When installed according to Ontario Building Code (OBC) standards with liquid water barriers and proper flashing details, modern EIFS acrylic stucco lasts 50+ years. The elastomeric acrylic finish coat resists freeze-thaw expansion, preventing the deep structural cracking commonly seen in older un-reinforced cement plaster.
