WHO WE ARE

NORTH YORK'S PREMIER ARCHITECTURAL EXTERIOR TEAM.

Reno Sons Stucco was founded on a simple commitment: delivering uncompromising exterior craftsmanship engineered specifically for Southern Ontario's climate.

From luxury custom residential estates in York Mills, Bridle Path, and Willowdale to high-impact commercial developments across North York and the Greater Toronto Area, our skilled tradespeople combine traditional European plastering techniques with modern acrylic chemical technology.

OUR CORE COMMITMENTS

  • Continuous Thermal R-Value Eliminating thermal bridges to cut heating & cooling costs by up to 30%.
  • 100% Acrylic Color pigments Fade-resistant, dirt-repellent, and crack-mitigated elastomeric surfaces.
  • On-Schedule Trade Execution Strict adherence to construction schedules, builder timelines, and OBC codes.
OUR APPROACH

PRECISION ENGINEERING.

Exterior finishing is not merely decorative; it is your building's primary defense shield against rain, snow, wind, and thermal expansion. Our multi-layer EIFS system ensures continuous moisture drainage and air-tight thermal performance.

MULTI-STAGE WATERPROOFING

Liquid-applied membrane seals substrates before any insulation board is installed.

FIBERGLASS REINFORCEMENT

High-tensile mesh embedded in polymer scratch coat resists severe impact and cracking.
